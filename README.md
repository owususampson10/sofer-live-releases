# Sofer Live

Live sermon transcription for the church media desk. Free, and it works offline.

Sofer Live listens to the preacher through the sound mixer and writes the sermon on screen as it
is spoken. The media operator copies any line into ProPresenter or EasyWorship with one click or
one hotkey. "Sofer" is Hebrew for scribe: the app listens and writes; a person decides what goes
on screen.

This page holds the installers. The app's code is kept elsewhere.

Older versions and what changed in each: [Releases](https://github.com/owususampson10/sofer-live-releases/releases).

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- A way to get sound from the mixer into the PC, such as a USB audio interface (any microphone
  works for trying it out)
- A graphics card is optional. Sofer Live uses one if the PC has it, and runs on the processor if
  not.

A macOS version is planned.

## Install

1. Download `Sofer-Live-Setup.exe` with the link above and open it.
2. Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"**. This happens because the installer isn't
   code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Follow the setup screen. It sets up the speech model the first time. After that, Sofer Live
   works with no internet.

## What it does

- Writes the sermon live, on the PC itself
- One-click copy of any line, plus hotkeys that work while ProPresenter or EasyWorship is in front
  (Ctrl+Alt+C copies the latest line)
- Marks points, quotes, declarations and prayer points, and builds an outline of the sermon
- Spots Bible references and readings and shows the verse (KJV or WEB, included)
- Church vocabulary list and spelling help for Bible names
- Optional online extras, off until you turn them on in Settings

## Privacy

Transcription happens on your PC. The sermon's audio isn't sent anywhere unless you turn on an
online feature. If "Save corrected lines for training" is on, short clips of lines you corrected
are kept on the PC only, and you can delete them any time in Settings › Privacy.
